CEO MESSAGE

CEO Message

A company that puts customer satisfaction first —
P&P Korea will build the future.

Since its founding in 2012, P&P Korea has achieved success in global business on the strength of skilled professionals and deep experience. By cooperating proactively on a foundation of mutual trust, we have built a base for communicating with our customers from a position of readiness. Our business spans the design and construction of power transmission parts (roller chain, elevator components, chemicals, etc.) and industrial equipment (automation and environmental facilities), along with the maintenance and improvement of existing public infrastructure.

We make building trust with customers our top priority and pursue the continuity of our business. Through new knowledge, new technology and capabilities suited to new environments, every member of our team will move forward with a more enterprising and sincere attitude. True to our name, we listen to the voice of the customer and take it to heart so that we can meet your expectations. Thank you for visiting our website. We will keep working to be a company that contributes to both our customers and society.

P&P Korea Co., Ltd. — the whole team
