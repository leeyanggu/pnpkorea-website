THE UNIT

It starts with a single container

Growing equipment and an integrated environmental control system are built into a 40-foot refrigerated container. It installs and operates immediately — no dedicated site or construction — and fits urban or unused land.

R&D

Joint research with the KIST Natural Product Institute

GreenCell has worked with the KIST Gangneung Institute of Natural Products on growing techniques that raise the antioxidant content of shiitake mushrooms. Following a technology-transfer ceremony in March 2022, a GreenCell unit was installed and is operated inside the institute, building up growing data.

Baekhwago

A top-grade shiitake whose cap surface cracks white. Grown to a uniform quality year-round in a controlled environment, raising both antioxidant content and texture.
