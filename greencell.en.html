WHY IT MATTERS

A future-ready system for standardizing and automating mushroom farming

Unlike field crops, shiitake (baekhwago) is highly sensitive to its environment and demanding to grow. Most mushroom smart farms have automated only the climate (temperature, humidity), while pinning, filling, thinning, quality control, pathogen (green mold) treatment and isolation, and harvest are still done by eye and by hand — leaving quality variance, rising labor cost and low substrate yield.

GreenCell was built to solve this: an “ICT-based automated shiitake (baekhwago) cultivation container-farm system” for premium-grade mushrooms. It reduces the usual 8–10 manual steps to 2–3, and integrates automated substrate transport, automated pinning and AI pathogen detection & isolation inside a single container.

STRUCTURE

Inside the container farm

Substrate pot (POT)
An improved rack-mounted pot that lets mushrooms be harvested from the top surface and water / nutrient be supplied. It sits on the rail and feeds the automated transport system.
Operator zone
Sawdust substrate circulates automatically to the operator, who thins and harvests from a seated position.
Elevator zone
Raises and re-circulates the substrate pots to match the operator's working height.
Inclined rail zone
An inclined rail circulates the substrate downward automatically — about 80% cheaper than a fully powered conveyor build.
Auto pinning zone
Doses water or nutrient in proportion to each substrate's weight.
Weighing zone
Weighs the substrate to calculate the required water / nutrient volume.

Dietary fiber DIETARY FIBER

As a low-calorie food rich in dietary fiber, shiitake supports stomach and intestinal function and may help with obesity, diabetes, heart disease and constipation.

Lentinan LENTINAN

Shiitake contains lentinan, an anti-tumor polysaccharide studied and developed as a compound that aids cancer treatment, boosts immunity and suppresses cancer-cell proliferation.

Vitamins B1 / B2 / D VITAMINS

Shiitake carries more than twice the B1, B2 and D of vegetables and, with eritadenine, helps strengthen bones, while vitamin D promotes calcium absorption.

Guanylic acid GUANYLIC ACID

A nucleotide seasoning compound behind the flavor. Added to cooking it brings umami, and it is processed as a natural seasoning; the aroma comes from lenthionine.

Eritadenine ERITADENINE

Works to clear cholesterol from the blood and lower blood pressure, which may help prevent hypertension.

Protein β-glucan BETA-GLUCAN

Interferon-inducing substances separate from shiitake spores; the double-stranded RNA among them has been reported to help prevent influenza-virus infection.

About Cheonhwago in particular

Dried shiitake has a stronger aroma than fresh, and when dried in natural sunlight the precursor (ergosterol) converts to vitamin D at 3–4 times the level. GreenCell adds antioxidant-enhancing cultivation techniques on top of this, further raising the good components shiitake already carries.

STORAGE

Storing Cheonhwago

Fresh shiitake — refrigerated

  • Rinse briefly under running water to remove soil and debris.
  • Pat dry gently with paper or kitchen towel.
  • Portion, wrap and keep in the vegetable drawer — stays fresh about 4–7 days.

Fresh shiitake — frozen

  • Blanch in boiling water for 1–2 minutes.
  • Cool rapidly in cold water.
  • Drain thoroughly in a sieve.
  • Pack in a freezer container — keeps about 1–2 months.

Points to watch

  • Humidity — shiitake is sensitive to moisture, so avoid humid places.
  • Sunlight — direct light can discolor it; store somewhere dark.
  • Odor — it absorbs surrounding smells easily, so keep it away from other ingredients.

Dried shiitake

  • Seal in an airtight container or bag so no air gets in, and keep refrigerated or frozen.
  • Direct sunlight can bring mushroom flies and other pests, so store away from light.
