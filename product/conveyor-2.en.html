Flow Conveyor Chain

Conveyor Chain

← Conveyor Chain

A dedicated chain for enclosed bulk conveyors that carry powders and granules inside a sealed case with no scattering. It is a standard conveyor chain fitted with attachments chosen to match the material.

It comes in horizontal, inclined/vertical and grain-handling versions, with the chain body standardised in normal, semi-heavy and heavy grades.

  • Enclosed-case transport — no dust scatter
  • Horizontal / inclined-vertical / grain-handling types
  • Choice of L, B, KL, U attachments

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
