DOUBLE PITCH CHAIN

Center Cover Chain

Double Pitch Chain

← Double Pitch Chain

A chain with a cover over the joints to keep out foreign objects such as bolts and nuts. It runs at a 1:1 speed ratio to the load, and its low centre of gravity carries goods stably.

Component coatings and other variations are available to suit the application.

  • Cover over joints keeps foreign objects out
  • Choice of roller material (steel / plastic)
  • Application-specific coatings and variants

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
