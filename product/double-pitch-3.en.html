DOUBLE PITCH CHAIN

Speed-Doubling (VR) Chain

Double Pitch Chain

← Double Pitch Chain

Friction between a large and a small roller turns them together, so the load is carried at roughly 2.5× the chain speed, set by the roller diameter ratio.

Roller material is chosen to suit the line — PLASTIC for low noise, STEEL for heavy loads.

  • Carry speed ≈ 2.5× chain speed
  • PLASTIC type (low noise) / STEEL type (heavy load)
  • Selection guide and examples available

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
