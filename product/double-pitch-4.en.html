DOUBLE PITCH CHAIN

Double Pitch Top Roller Chain

Double Pitch Chain

← Double Pitch Chain

A carrying-purpose double-pitch chain that moves goods supported directly on large top rollers.

On long-centre lines the load rides on the top rollers above the chain; roller material and attachments are set to suit the line.

  • Large top-roller carrying construction
  • For long-centre conveyors
  • Choice of roller material and attachments
