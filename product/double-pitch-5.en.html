DOUBLE PITCH CHAIN

Double Pitch Roller Chain

Double Pitch Chain

← Double Pitch Chain

A chain with a link-plate pitch twice that of a standard chain — an economical choice for conveyor lines with long shaft centres.

It comes with S-type or R-type rollers, and can carry a range of attachments — A1, A2, G, K1, K2, SA1, SA2, SK1, SK2 and more.

  • Pitch twice that of a standard chain
  • S-type / R-type rollers
  • A1, A2, G, K1, K2, SA, SK attachments

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
