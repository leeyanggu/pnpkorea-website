Engineering Plastic

A high-performance plastic that can replace metal and ceramics. We supply lube-free chains and parts with high strength and elasticity that hold up at elevated temperatures.

TP-LUBE 610 is slow-moulded from refined AP-lactam with the special additive LUBE, giving about 20× the durability of MC nylon and a friction coefficient of 0.03–0.08. Continuous service temperature is 160°C (heat-deflection 210°C); it is used for wear plates, gears, sprockets, rollers, bearings and guides.

  • TP-LUBE 610 / TP-LUBE 601 / MC NYLON / 4F (PTFE)
  • Continuous service 160°C (4F: 260°C)
  • Wear plates, gears, sprockets, rollers, sheaves and more
