Roller Chain

← Roller Chain

A chain made with special materials and processes to give higher tensile and fatigue strength than a standard chain.

It is applied to power-transmission drives and safety-critical equipment such as lifts, where high fatigue strength is required.

  • Higher tensile and fatigue strength than standard
  • Applied to lifts and safety-critical equipment
  • For high-load power transmission
