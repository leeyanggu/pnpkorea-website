ROLLER CHAIN

Hollow Pin Chain

Roller Chain

← Roller Chain

A chain joined with hollow pins bored through the centre. Using these pins, attachments can be added or removed while the chain stays in place.

Its pitch, roller diameter and roller-link inner width match the standard roller chain and double-pitch chain, so standard sprockets can be used as is.

  • Hollow pins make attachments easy to fit and remove
  • Standard sprockets can be used
  • Compatible with standard-pitch and double-pitch chains

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
