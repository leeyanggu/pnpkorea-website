ROLLER CHAIN

Top Roller Chain

Roller Chain

← Roller Chain

A free-flow chain with the RS chain plates extended upward to carry a freely rotating top roller.

Goods loaded on the top rollers can be moved independently of the chain's direction and speed, so it is used to hold items with a stopper, or to run conveying and stopping on the same chain at once.

  • Rotating top-roller construction
  • Conveying and stopping independent of chain travel
  • Suits lines that use stoppers
