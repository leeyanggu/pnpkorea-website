ROLLER CHAIN

Side Roller Chain

Roller Chain

← Roller Chain

A free-flow chain that conveys or accumulates goods carried on top of the chain. With many side rollers fitted, small items can be carried directly on the chain itself.

By roller layout it is either the Z type (staggered) or the K type (in line), and it is built in both standard-pitch and double-pitch versions.

  • Z type (staggered) / K type (in line)
  • Standard-pitch type / double-pitch type
  • For free-flow conveying and accumulation lines

Part numbers and special specifications not shown here can also be made. Tell us your operating conditions and part number and we will confirm the right product.
