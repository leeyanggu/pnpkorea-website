The roller chain is the standard chain most widely used for power transmission. It is generally marked "RS", and depending on the brand it may be written HC (Korea Chain) or DBC (Dongbo Chain) — e.g. RS40-1 = HC40-1.

We cover the full range from single strand to multi-strand, RS40 through RS240.

  • 1 pitch = 3.175 mm
  • Single, double, triple and other multi-strand builds
  • Full range RS40 – RS240
